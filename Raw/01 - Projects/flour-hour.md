# Flour Hour (bakery)

> EXAMPLE CONTENT, fictional. Replace with your own project notes.

## Status, January 2026

Six years in. Revenue steady, growth flat. Two employees (Sam on counter, Dee on pastry). Best sellers: sourdough, morning buns. Worst margin: custom cakes, they eat entire afternoons.

## The loyalty card problem

Punch cards keep failing. People forget them, Sam forgets to punch them, the print shop misprinted the last batch. Redemption rate is maybe 15 percent, so the program costs money without changing behavior.

Ideas kicked around:

- Phone-number based points at the register (no app, no card)
- "First visit free coffee" instead of a ten-visit ladder, because the data says a second visit within two weeks predicts a regular, and nothing else does
- Kill loyalty entirely and put the budget into the Saturday sample table

## Decision

Try the phone-number points for one quarter. The insight that matters: **the reward has to land at the moment of the behavior we want, not ten visits later.** A distant reward is invisible.

## Open questions

- Does the register software export visit data?
- What does Dee think? She talks to more regulars than anyone.
