# Moonpatch (indie game)

> EXAMPLE CONTENT, fictional. Replace with your own project notes.

## What it is

A cozy 2D gardening game where every plant only grows at night. You plan the garden by day, then the night phase runs itself and surprises you at dawn. Building it with AI assistance in a free engine, nights and weekends.

## Status, January 2026

Core loop playable: plant, sleep, wake, harvest. Ugly art, placeholder sounds. Three playtesters (Sam, Dee, and my nephew).

## The tutorial problem

All three playtesters got lost in the first five minutes. I wrote a tutorial that explains everything up front: seed types, moisture, moon phases, the compost system. Nephew's review: "it is a lot of reading before the fun part."

Rewrite plan: teach ONE thing, let them feel the night-growth payoff immediately, then introduce the second system only when the player hits the wall that system solves. **The payoff has to land at the moment of the behavior I want to reinforce (planting), not after a lecture.**

Noticing this is the same shape as the bakery loyalty conclusion. Filing that thought here so it does not evaporate.
