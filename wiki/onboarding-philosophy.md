# Onboarding Philosophy

**Summary**: Cross-domain sequencing rule: teach one thing, pay it off immediately, and introduce each further system only when the user hits the wall that system solves.

**Sources**: Raw/01 - Projects/moonpatch.md, Raw/01 - Projects/flour-hour.md

**Last updated**: 2026-01-20

---

This is the demonstration page for why cross-linking is the whole point of the wiki. Neither source note is "about" onboarding, yet together they define a philosophy:

- The [[moonpatch]] tutorial rewrite: one mechanic (plant a seed), immediate payoff (night growth), every other system deferred until its wall appears. Compost cut from v1 entirely.
- The [[flour-hour]] points rollout: a new customer learns exactly one thing ("give your phone number"), gets paid off fast (coffee near visit two), and never needs the whole rules sheet.

The shared skeleton: **a first-run experience is a promise, and the promise must be kept before the second lesson.**
