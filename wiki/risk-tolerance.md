# Risk Tolerance

**Summary**: The numbers that bound Riley's decisions: business 7, finance 3, creative 9, plus the 200-dollar sleep-on-it rule. The agent cites these instead of guessing.

**Sources**: Raw/00 - Core/riley-profile.md, Raw/02 - Calibration/2026-01-15 - reward timing qa.md

**Last updated**: 2026-01-20

---

From the core profile (source: Raw/00 - Core/riley-profile.md):

| Domain | Tolerance (0 to 10) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Business experiments | 7 | will trial a product line on a hunch |
| Finance | 3 | the year-two near-fold is a permanent scar |
| Creative | 9 | the game can be weird, that is the point |

**The sleep-on-it rule:** any spend over 200 dollars waits one night. Riley confirmed it applies to everything, including game asset packs: "It applies to everything with my card number on it." (source: Raw/02 - Calibration/2026-01-15 - reward timing qa.md)

## How the agent uses this

When proposing options, price them and check the number against this table BEFORE presenting. A 9-rated creative swing and a 3-rated financial commitment are different conversations even when they cost the same. The low finance number is a scar, not timidity; do not argue with it, design around it.
