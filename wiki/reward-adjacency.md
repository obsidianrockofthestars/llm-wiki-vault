# Reward Adjacency

**Summary**: Standing principle, ratified verbatim by Riley on 2026-01-15: "Reward adjacency beats reward size." The reward must land at the moment of the behavior it reinforces, not later.

**Sources**: Raw/02 - Calibration/2026-01-15 - reward timing qa.md, Raw/01 - Projects/flour-hour.md, Raw/01 - Projects/moonpatch.md

**Last updated**: 2026-01-20

---

Riley, verbatim: "If the good thing happens far away from the action, the action never repeats. Customer buys a bun, reward them now. Player plants a seed, show them the magic tonight." (source: Raw/02 - Calibration/2026-01-15 - reward timing qa.md)

This page exists because the same conclusion was reached independently in two domains in the same week, which is exactly the signal the wiki is built to catch:

- **[[flour-hour]]**: free coffee near visit two beats free cake at visit ten. A distant reward is invisible.
- **[[moonpatch]]**: the planting action pays off the same night, before any second system is taught.

## Application test

Before shipping any incentive, ask: how many actions sit between the behavior and its reward? If the answer is more than one, the incentive probably does not exist from the user's point of view.
