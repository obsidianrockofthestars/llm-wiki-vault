Six years in, two employees (Sam on counter, Dee on pastry). Best sellers are sourdough and morning buns; custom cakes have the worst margin and eat afternoons (source: Raw/01 - Projects/flour-hour.md).

## The loyalty redesign

Punch cards failed on every axis: forgotten cards, missed punches, a misprint, roughly 15 percent redemption. Decision: phone-number points at the register, one-quarter trial, no app.

The reasoning is the durable part: the data says a second visit within two weeks predicts a regular, and nothing else does. So the reward moves to the moment of the behavior, a free coffee near visit two, not a free item at visit ten. This is the origin of [[reward-adjacency]], now a standing principle also steering [[moonpatch]]'s tutorial.

The trial spend cleared the sleep-on-it threshold in [[risk-tolerance]].
